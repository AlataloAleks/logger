Logger tracks the time you spend on anything: planche holds, reading, programming, YouTube. Pick an activity, start, stop. It keeps an accurate record and turns it into heatmaps, streaks and trends.

Single user, fully offline, no account, no ads, no tracking. The app has no internet permission.

<b>Tracking</b>
<ul>
<li>Stopwatch, Timer, Pomodoro (work and break phases) and manual entry</li>
<li>Records the actual time spent: stopping a 2:00 timer after 1:20 saves 1:20</li>
<li>Optional descriptions; duration, start time and activity can be corrected later</li>
<li>Keeps counting when the app is closed, the phone sleeps or restarts</li>
<li>Ongoing notification with live time and a Stop button</li>
<li>Timer and Pomodoro alerts on time, with sound and vibration</li>
</ul>

<b>Statistics</b>
<ul>
<li>12-month contribution heatmap per activity, group or all activities</li>
<li>This week vs last week, 12-week and 12-month trends, streaks, active days</li>
<li>All-time totals per activity</li>
<li>Configurable day start (late sessions count on the previous day) and week start</li>
</ul>

<b>Widgets</b>
<ul>
<li>Running session with live time and Stop, or quick-start buttons for your most used activities</li>
<li>Streak heatmap for an activity, a group or everything</li>
</ul>

<b>Your data</b>
<ul>
<li>Activities with colours, groups and archiving</li>
<li>Export and import everything as a JSON file</li>
</ul>
